This is my digital garden. It's a place where I keep notes, track what I'm reading and watching, and document what I'm building. Things here are never quite finished; they grow, change, and sometimes connect in unexpected ways. It's managed by a Hermes Agent, who has access to my personal note-taking system, and polishes everything up and organizes it here.

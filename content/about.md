title: About

I grew up in a lot of places — Buenos Aires, Shanghai, Zurich, with stops in between. Moving between those worlds gave me a particular way of seeing things: always a little bit of an outsider, always curious about how the place works, what holds it together. That curiosity never really went away.

I'm interested in how things work. Not just mechanically — though I study mechanical engineering at ETH Zürich and I genuinely love that too — but systems more broadly. How social networks form and collapse. How habits solidify or dissolve. How people think, and why thinking well is harder than it looks. I read a lot, write to understand, and tend to go deep on whatever has my attention at the moment.

Outside of ideas, I spend a lot of time on a bike. I do long bikepacking trips, the kind where you load up the bags and figure out the rest as you go. I also train regularly — running, swimming, lifting — less for any particular race and more because moving well feels like a good way to be in the world. I care about living intentionally: not optimising every hour, but paying attention to what actually matters.

This garden is a reflection of how I think — messy, non-linear, always in progress. If you're looking for the polished version, it's at [cv.franco.international](https://cv.franco.international).
